# Sustainashare — Funder / investor one-pager

*Plain-language briefing for diligence conversations. Not a formal prospectus. Claims are limited to what the product is designed to do.*

---

## One sentence

**Sustainashare is a multi-sided redistribution orchestrator:** we help communities move usable surplus to verified need, complete safe handovers, and count **only confirmed impact** — we are not a shop, not a courier company, and not a surveillance network.

---

## Problem

Usable surplus (food and other resources) coexists with unmet local need. Informal channels (WhatsApp, ad-hoc drop-offs) struggle with:

- trust and quality verification  
- privacy and dignity for people in need  
- reliable last-mile handover  
- honest proof of what actually reached someone  

Institutions (hotels, retailers, NGOs, CSR programmes) also lack a shared system that produces **board-ready evidence** without turning redistribution into retail.

---

## Solution (platform journey)

```text
List surplus → Verify → Match to need → Claim/reserve
       → Volunteer or partner handover → Recipient confirms
       → Verified impact record only
```

**Product role:** coordination, verification workflow, privacy boundaries, audit trail, and impact evidence.  
**Physical movement:** volunteers and institutional logistics partners — orchestrated in-product, not owned as a fleet by default.

---

## Category and non-goals

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Redistribution operating system | Online shop / checkout marketplace |
| Trust + matching + fulfilment workflow | Opaque “AI decides who deserves help” |
| Verified-impact evidence layer | Invented meal/carbon counters |
| Multi-sided: donor, recipient, volunteer, admin, org | Public recipient profiles or leaderboards |
| Light logistics **enablement** | Default own-fleet logistics company |
| B2B/institution software + mission funding | Charging people in need to receive support |

## Who participates / who pays

| Side | Role on platform | Pays? |
|------|------------------|-------|
| Individual donors | List surplus | Free (core loop) |
| Recipients | State needs; confirm receipt | **Never charged for access to help** |
| Volunteers | Accept and complete handovers | Free; optional stipend funded by partners/programmes |
| Organisations (hotels, retail, NGOs, CBOs) | Bulk surplus, membership, coordination | **Primary commercial payer** (seats, ops, SLAs) |
| CSR / foundations / public programmes | Credible redistribution + reports | **Impact packs / grants / programme contracts** |

**Monetisation principle:** sell reliability, coordination, audit, and verified reporting to institutions — keep the community redistribution journey free and non-commercial.

Illustrative packaging (to be priced per market):

1. **Community** — free individual journeys, verified impact only  
2. **Organisation** — multi-user, bulk listings, moderation support, audit  
3. **Impact / partner** — exports, methodology notes, priority verification, SLA

## Logistics model (diligence answer)

We **orchestrate** capacity; we do not need to own vans to be a viable platform.

| Mode | Who moves goods | When |
|------|-----------------|------|
| **A. Community volunteers** | Local volunteers with service area + capacity | Small loads, short distance, dense coverage |
| **B. Partner logistics** | NGO / CBO / programme already doing last-mile | Bulk, recurring, or sensitive handovers |
| **C. Contract courier** (later) | Paid riders under a funded contract | Only when an organisation pays for movement |
| **D. Own fleet** | Sustainashare | Not the seed default |

**Policy:** failed handovers do not become fake impact. If capacity is missing, fulfilment does not complete. Bulk and long-distance work defaults to **partners**, not random volunteers.

Exact handover details are revealed only after authorised assignment (privacy by design).

---

## Differentiation

- **State-machine journey** — surplus is not “sold”; it moves through verified statuses.  
- **Explainable matching** — rules and reasons, not a black box.  
- **Privacy** — approximate service areas; no public recipient spectacle.  
- **Impact honesty** — counts only after recipient confirmation (or equivalent verified completion).  
- **Official context ≠ our KPI** — national statistics (e.g. World Bank undernourishment) may inform context; they are not Sustainashare impact.

---

## North-star metric

**Verified successful fulfilments per active service area per week.**

Supporting signals (secondary): time-to-match, assignment acceptance rate, repeat participation, active verified partner organisations — never vanity inventory or unverified “meals served.”

## Use of funds (typical ask narrative)

Capital is for **density and trust**, not a vehicle fleet on day one:

1. County ops — onboarding donors, volunteers, and partners  
2. Verification / moderation capacity  
3. Product hardening (reliability, privacy, reporting)  
4. Compliance and safety (food quality guidance, data protection)  
5. Optional: funded courier integrations **only after** paying logistics demand exists  

---

## Key risks (acknowledged)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Last-mile no-shows | Service-area capacity gates; partner backup for bulk |
| Quality / safety of surplus | Listing standards, admin verification before “available” |
| Overclaimed impact | Confirm-only impact records; methodology discipline |
| Trust / privacy failure | RBAC, redaction, no public recipient profiles |
| Premature national scale | County-first expansion |

---

## Ask of the funder / investor

Partnership that values **measurable redistribution infrastructure**: software, local operating density, and honest evidence — not retail GMV or vanity reach.

**Conversation close:**  
“We build the rails for surplus to become verified support. Institutions pay for coordination and proof; communities keep a free, dignified journey; logistics is partnered capacity we orchestrate — not a fleet we pretend to own before the economics exist.”
